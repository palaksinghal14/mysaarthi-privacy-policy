Privacy Policy — MySaarthi

Last updated: October 2, 2026

MySaarthi ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal spiritual-companion app built to support your Bhagwat Marg practice. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using MySaarthi, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and email address, collected when you register using Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile information: details you choose to add during onboarding or in your profile, such as your practice preferences and reminder times.
  • Location information: if you turn on the "Use my location" option, we collect your approximate device location so we can show nearby temples and, if you opt in, help other seekers find you for satsang. Location is stored as a truncated, low-precision geohash (roughly 1.2 km accuracy), never your exact coordinates. You can turn this off at any time in Settings or Edit Profile.
  • Usage data: basic in-app activity needed to make features like reminders, streaks, and satsang connections work.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To show you nearby temples and, if enabled, nearby seekers for satsang.
  • To schedule and deliver your daily practice reminders.
  • To keep your profile and progress in sync across sessions.
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is only shared in the following limited ways:

  • With other users, if you opt in: if you turn on "Open to Satsang," your approximate (truncated) location and profile name become visible to nearby users so they can send you a satsang request.If you accept a satsang request, your email address is shared with that user so you can connect directly.
  • With service providers: we use trusted infrastructure providers (see Section 4) strictly to operate the app — they do not use your data for their own purposes.
  • For legal reasons: if required to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request.

4. Third-Party Services

MySaarthi is built using the following third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Authentication — manages sign-up and login.
  • Cloud Firestore — securely stores your profile, preferences, and satsang data.
  • Google Maps & Places API — powers the nearby-temples map and search.

We do not currently use Firebase Cloud Messaging or any other push-notification or advertising service.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This action is irreversible and removes your profile, preferences, and satsang connections from our systems.

6. Children's Privacy

MySaarthi is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly design features for, or knowingly collect personal information from, young children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including Firebase's authentication and Firestore security rules, which restrict access so that only you can read or modify your own data (except for the limited, opt-in satsang-visibility described above). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using commercially reasonable means.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
