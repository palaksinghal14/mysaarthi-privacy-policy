Delete Your MySaarthi Account

MySaarthi ("we", "our", "the app") provides the following ways to request deletion of your account and all associated data.

Option 1: Delete directly within the app

  1. Open the MySaarthi app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Option 2: Request deletion if you no longer have the app

Email us at palak.singhal.builds@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your MySaarthi account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted: Your profile, name, email, practice preferences, reminder settings, location data, and satsang connections are permanently removed from our systems. This action is irreversible.
